Game Rules

  1. Place up to 3 pieces each (X starts).
  2. After placing 3, you move your own piece to an empty cell.
  3. If you do nothing, your last piece is pre-selected. Click another to switch.
  4. First to align 3 in a row wins.
  5. Each player has a total timer. If yours hits 0, you lose.
