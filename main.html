Game Rules

  1. Place up to 3 pieces each (X starts).
  2. After placing 3, you move your own piece to an empty cell (select, then tap a target).
  3. If you do nothing, your last piece is pre-selected for you. Click another to switch.
  4. First to align a row/column/diagonal of 3 wins the round.
  5. Each player has a 3:00 total timer. If yours hits 0, you lose the round.

Optional: enable adjacency rule in Settings to only move to neighboring cells.
